How the numbers are built

Each scenario is a full 8,760-hour year. Generation comes from the 2025 and 2026 meter data mapped onto 2027 dates. 2026 HES output has the water spilled during near-zero PTF hours added back, because the plants ran at capacity just before and after those stops.

Every deal is settled against hourly PTF: revenue = own generation × PTF + contracted MWh × (deal price − PTF). When the portfolio produces less than the contracted MW, the gap is bought at PTF. HES is stopped whenever PTF falls below the stop price, even with a deal running, because buying the cover is cheaper than generating.

Fair value = scenario-weighted average PTF over the hours a deal covers. Value vs fair = (deal price − fair value) × contracted MWh, which is what the deal adds to or takes from expected revenue. Its other job is to lift the worst case.
